# Independent fixed-weight finite controls checker v1

Before weighted research, authenticate all frozen source/build/tool/environment,
command/resource/outcome/cleanup receipts and the complete 32-call population.
Exactly 16 successful calls and 16 deliberate malformed calls are expected.
No native producer or solver is invoked or imported by this checker. Earlier
independently calibrated unweighted domain/parsing/RNG/matrix helpers are shared
with an exact source pin and disclosed; the changed weighted scorer/parser and
replay are independently written.

Reconstruct the complete adjacency sets from labelled linear triples and derive
E_lambda and E_mu by full pair intersections and actual adjacency categories.
Derive F=6E_lambda+E_mu afresh for each current/proposed graph, without using the
native incremental formula. Replay every one of the 19,456 proposed trades,
their exact RNG words, admissibility, integer deltas, component scores, accepted
or rolled back state, weighted best selection and counters. Check all actual
saved checkpoints and both complete raw current/best matrix exports. Check all
86 pair-cost algebra records per success (1,376 total).

Import exactly the pinned old unweighted current or best labelled graph, check
all of its old ordinary score/cache/domain fields, and check reset counters,
new seed/config/RNG and recomputed components. Resume must preserve every raw
checkpoint byte/configuration; both fresh and imported whole/split final states
and all trace records must be identical. Wrong graph selection, retained prior
counters/RNG, wrong selector and use of an old unweighted checkpoint as weighted
resume are independent negative controls. Native malformed calls must have
their exact expected stderr, exit2, empty stdout and no scientific artifact;
independently parse the exact corrupted state and require the specified failure
stage. Unrelated exceptions do not calibrate rejection.

Positive controls include known exact rook9 (all81 integer matrix identities),
its rejection as a99certificate, corrupted matrix edge, and exact initial99
component values. Changed trace/delta/component/RNG/weight and pair-cost records,
including the old unweighted edge category formula, must fail. On the declared
simple regular graph domain, positive integer weights make F0 equivalent to all
off-diagonal CN=2-A; regularity supplies the diagonal. F0 is therefore exactly
the SRG identity, but no graph from these finite controls is a target resolution.

Temperature absolute tolerance and strict acceptance probability margin are
both1e-12; counts/energies/deltas/RNG/cache are exact integers. All controls must
pass with no skipped attempts. Scope is finite engineering calibration only:
no performance, move connectivity, search coverage, exclusion or general
correctness theorem is claimed. Weighted F and ordinary E remain distinct.

internal output reserve. Every preprocessing hash/read/control/check and child
shares the allowance; no scientific child is launched. Earlier unweighted full
replay29seconds supports the larger doubled proposal/state population. Save all
