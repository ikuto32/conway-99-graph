## Exact mathematical problem and dual certificate

Define K_j(i) as the coefficient of z^j in

    (1+(q-1)z)^(n-i) (1-z)^i.

Equivalently it is the exact integer sum over ell of
(-1)^ell (q-1)^(j-ell) binom(i,ell) binom(n-i,j-ell), with only valid
binomial indices. For every linear q-ary code and its dual,

    sum_i A_i K_j(i) = M B_j.

Here is the required normalization derivation. For a nontrivial additive
character psi, sum_(x in C) psi(u dot x) is M if u is in C-perp and zero
otherwise, by translating C by a word with a nontrivial character value.
For fixed x, summing psi(u dot x) over words u of weight j gives K_j(wt x):
an x-zero coordinate contributes q-1 when chosen and a nonzero coordinate
contributes sum_(a!=0)psi(a x)=-1. Summing in the two orders proves the identity.
No eigenspace, equitable profile, graph automorphism or extra left word is used.

For degree h=8 or12, let ell_j=0 except the optional four image lower rows.
A submitted candidate consists of h canonical rational multipliers y_j>=0.
It must satisfy, exactly at all45 allowed nonzero weights i=55..99,

    f(i)=1+sum_(j=1..h)y_j(K_j(i)-ell_j) <=0.

Then sum_i A_i f(i)=M+sum_j y_j M(B_j-ell_j)>=M, while A_0=1 and the
nonzero-weight terms are nonpositive, so M<=f(0). The proposed dimension
