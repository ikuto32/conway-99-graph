## Literal model and why its constraints are necessary

Order all exterior copy labels lexicographically, excluding exactly x. There
are81 selector variables q in scientific mode, including unavailable copies
fixed to zero. A selector is binary. Let U_w be its support type. Its lower
bound is1 exactly when the focal pair admits only bit1. Its upper bound is1
exactly when bit1 is permitted and |T intersect U_w|<=1. Thus a forced neighbor
with focal overlap>=2 creates an explicit lower1/upper0 contradiction.

Retain all18 common-neighbor/degree equalities:

* sum q_w =14-|T|;
* for each u in S, sum_{w: u in U_w} q_w =2-1_T(u)-(H t)_u.

For u in T, independence gives right side1. Together with the focal-overlap
restriction, exactly one selected w partners each u. Every selected w meeting
T once therefore lies in P, already uses its unique edge in N(x) to T, and
has no W edge. Copies meeting T twice or more cannot be selected.

Matching variables m are binary unordered pairs of literal copy indices, only
when both endpoint types are disjoint from T, their support intersection is
empty and their pair bits permit1. For every T-disjoint candidate w, impose
sum of incident m =q_w. Every selected R vertex has exactly one partner; every
unselected one has none. Also write both explicit endpoint inequalities m<=q.
No matching variables exist at P endpoints or at suppressed-overlap endpoints.

For every pair of distinct available copies, including equal-type distinct
copies, apply exactly these rules:

* Bit0 (empty allowed-bit list) forbids q_w=q_z=1, even when the support
  intersection is0 or1. Gram-only incompatibility is not omitted.
* Support intersection>=2 also forbids joint selection, independently of bits.
* Sole bit1 requires the matching edge when an eligible m exists:
  q_w+q_z-m_wz<=1. If no eligible m exists, joint selection is prohibited.
* Sole bit0, represented by [0], admits only nonadjacency and never creates m.
* Bits[0,1] allow either, subject to the matching and support restrictions.

All other W edges are absent by definition of the local witness. Consequently
the supplied pair bits are enforced on every selected pair, P has no W edges,
and R induces exactly a perfect matching. Repeated selected types of support
size>=2 are excluded by their pair overlap; empty/singleton types have no false
global or focal unit-count rule. Constraints are necessary for an actual focal
neighborhood and sufficient for the specified1-regular N(x) construction.
They are not sufficient for all common-neighbor equations on S union W, a
symmetric82-vertex exterior block, a full99 graph or target resolution.

Rows are sparse integer terms and exact finite lower/upper bounds, with null
meaning the relevant unbounded side. Selector bounds and all rows are checked
over integers after numerical guidance. There is no float tolerance on the
final witness. The first exact interval bound record uses coefficient signs;
