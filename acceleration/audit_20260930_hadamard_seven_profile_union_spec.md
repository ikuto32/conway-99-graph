# Conditional seven-profile union audit

Freeze this protocol before executing preparation. No native solver, DRAT process,
ledger or publication operation is permitted. The two modes have different claims.

`prepare --out NEW` authenticates the existing complete rank/DP/local-domain/AC/
normalization/encoding premises, reconstructs all 1,608 literal profiles and six
fibre actions, checks the disjoint 312 plus 1,296 partition, and binds all 216 exact
formula scopes. It also authenticates the earlier literal0001 complete proof.
It produces no mathematical exclusion claim and no claim bindings. All 215 later
proofs remain a required premise even if preparation succeeds.

`final --proof-gate PATH --proof-gate-sha256 HASH --out NEW` repeats preparation
and requires the complete independently replayed215-proof report, exact selected
order, no unknown/SAT/unattempted cases, and VERIFIED/CLEAR revision1 binding for
the literal215 exclusion. Every actual CNF/model/scope and complete raw proof is
hashed; every independent checker receipt and acceptance log is authenticated.
It does not rerun DRAT. Missing raw artifacts are a failure, not an assumed pass.

The fixed population is all 1,608 necessary exactly-seven profiles produced by the
separately reviewed complete200-septet marginal census. Each profile has seven
initial local domains. The expected proof representatives are the216 nonempty
orbit minima, exactly the previously proved `rank5_07_profile_0001` plus all215
in the frozen run02 selection. Nonempty AC is never treated as feasibility.

Controls: actual partition and inverse actions; known small disjoint partition;
literal marginal equations; exact integer exception-case partition; synthetic
complete receipt metadata (explicitly not a proof); missing/duplicate reps,
overlap/gap, changed AC/universe identity, nonbijection, unaccepted/wrong proof
binding, altered scope/pruning/count bounds, unknown/SAT/unattempted/incomplete
terminal metadata. No floating arithmetic or acceptance tolerance is used.

Final statements concern only this literal fixed-support factor family with full
prescribed integer Gram and all outside-column caps. The new exactly-seven
exclusion combines with the pinned at-most-six exclusion to imply at least eight
unbalanced groups. Neither claims nonexistence of all fixed-support factors, the
core, or Conway99. Fibre transformations relabel solutions; no target automorphism
is assumed. Prior six-union source informs the implementation, but is not imported.

All outputs use fresh directories and retain the exact command, source commit,
Python version and input/output hashes. Preparation is bounded to one pass over
the saved profile/formula artifacts; final additionally hashes complete proofs.
No retry or newly weakened requirement is authorized by a failed check.
