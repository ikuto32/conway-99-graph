# Exactly-eight simultaneous Gram campaign: frozen selection and acceptance plan

Prepared after the complete scalar/block screens and the first next-profile
literal UNSAT pilot, before any campaign instance is built or solved.

Question: which of the 792 canonical count profiles surviving the complete
necessary screens admit a simultaneous integer Gram factor on the literal
six-prism Hadamard support, with the complete initial within-triplicate domains?
No target automorphism is assumed. The prescribed support/core and exactly
eight unbalanced groups are restrictions, not normalizations of the target.

Universe: the complete surviving representative stream bound by the independent
block gate `6c21ef6951f72fbeabab8be0a649f178ca8b17eb91edcc35c3291d6790f6256a`
and producer summary `9fbfde4c8d1f1b4fc0aee7b89783a76dcd71c48adf75dbe6c32f116438634edb`.
Freeze all 792 raw count tables and their hashes in a campaign manifest. Order
them by `(subset_index, canonical full-count hash)`. Retain historical profiles;
do not subtract older exclusions or rely on an unchecked union calculation.

First batch: take the first canonical profile from each of the first 12 distinct
surviving subset indices in that order. This is a scope-diverse pilot, not a
random sample. An earlier literal pilot may be repeated; count it as a new
attempt on the same distinct case, not a new case. Subsequent batches use the
same frozen universe in the stated order, skipping only independently completed
campaign cases with unchanged exact inputs. A changed encoding is a new attempt.

Each formula uses all initial local options, exact one-of-domain selection and
the complete prescribed integer Gram matrix. It omits cross-triplicate column
caps and the residual matrix D. There is no activity/arc-consistency reduction
of local options. Preserve the full raw model, local domains, scope, CNF and
exact command/source/dependency pins. Compress large originals losslessly and
check recovery; a hash alone is not evidence availability.

Before native calls, independently reconstruct each selected formula and its
entire local option set. Calibrate the generic object checker on a genuine
known-valid fixture and malformed objects, and bind the native wrapper source.
SAT is accepted only after checking the complete decoded raw factor against
this scope through the independent path. Then test all omitted column caps
and assess the residual-completion problem. A factor is not a target graph.
Pause automatic batch expansion on SAT to inspect that stronger lead.

UNSAT counts only after independent replay of the entire retained proof against
the exact checked CNF, with checker/source/version pins and corrupted controls.
Proof failure, timeout, resource stop and incomplete traces remain UNKNOWN or
verification pending. Preserve them. Do not infer exclusion from a failed run.

Per native attempt: 60 wall seconds, 1,000,000 conflicts, 4 GiB address-space
limit and 256 MiB proof-file limit. Use the recorded native defaults and retain
the actual random-seed setting. First-batch total native wall allowance is
720 seconds plus launch/verification overhead. Native calls are sequential.
Campaign resource ceiling is 64 GiB of newly retained raw solver artifacts;
require at least 32 GiB host and 2 GiB ext4 free before each attempt. A limit
causes a saved checkpoint and explicit reassessment, not a mathematical verdict.
Use tqdm progress and per-case resumable checkpoints. No floating-point
acceptance thresholds apply: all object/proof criteria are exact.

Before claiming all exactly-eight cases excluded, independently check the
frozen 792-case coverage, the earlier complete scalar exclusions, all six global
fibre images and their preservation of Gram/domain constraints. Do not simply
multiply case counts. No whole-support result follows from excluding exactly
eight unbalanced groups; larger counts remain. A new lower bound also requires
the previously verified zero-through-seven coverage as pinned premises.

Record any selection, formula, resource or acceptance change in a new plan
revision before the affected run. Root owns launches and claim registration.
