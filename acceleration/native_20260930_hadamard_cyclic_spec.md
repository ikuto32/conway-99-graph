Question: does the exact saved six-prism Hadamard support admit a prescribed-
Gram factor with all1770column caps, under the extra condition that each
triplet of identical coordinate supports uses cyclic shifts of one balanced
fibre coloring? This is a restricted partial-factor construction. Arbitrary
residualD remains unencoded; no automorphism of a target graph is assumed.

Selection follows the five frozen support scouts: one was excluded by a
local pigeonhole proof and three by independently checked integer Farkas
certificates. The remaining support has only a continuous fractional witness.
This next experiment tests the smaller cyclic subfamily before the larger
unrestricted-coloring model on the same fixed support.
