# Independent frozen-root two-line census kernel V1

This unexecuted independent scorer imports no producer. A separate caller must
bind the changed producer/checker bytes and fresh calibrated controls before
an actual census audit. This source is not a gate or a completed census result.

For one complete ordered linear3-uniform incidence list, independently rebuild
the integer adjacency bitmasks, exact point degrees, every baseline pair CN and
the baseline E_lambda, E_mu and Rroot. Define frozen labels as exactly those
literal lines containing the root; mutable labels are their ordered complement.
The labelled universe is every mutable i<j and every ordered position pair
(ix,jy) in {0,1,2}². The target population is224choose2*9=224784 labels.
These are labelled attempts, not distinct candidate graphs.

Each proposal swaps two selected exclusive points. It is invalid when either
selected point belongs to the other old line, or a proposed line pair is
already present after deleting both old lines. Exclusive points guarantee that
both new lines have three distinct points and still intersect in at most the
old single common point; the checker explicitly checks that premise. Pair-set
differences construct the new exact adjacency, retaining common old/new pairs.
No frozen line participates and the root adjacency mask must remain literal.

Let U be the endpoints of changed adjacency pairs. For u,v outside U, both
adjacency rows and their intersection are unchanged; thus the adjacency status
and CN cost for {u,v} are unchanged. Recompute CN as popcount(new_row_u AND
new_row_v) for every unordered pair meeting U, visiting every such pair once.
Replace its old exact edge/nonedge cost in the independently computed baseline.
The resulting E_lambda and E_mu are exact sums over all unordered vertex pairs.
Rroot is separately recomputed over every fixed root nonneighbor, irrespective
of the affected-pair cost computation. Froot=60E_lambda+Rroot. This independent
checking path does not import or reuse the producer's cached delta update.

Before a full audit, a new caller must compare all admissible proposals against
fresh full adjacency-set/scalar matrix calculations on the complete rook9 and
prism9 mutable universes (54 labels each) and the regular12 degree2 fixture
(135 labels), plus a declared target99 sample. Check frozen predicates, raw
typed record populations, labels, classifications, deltas, minimum/all-tie
selection, compressed part identities and exact row order. Precise corrupted
controls must reject wrong Rroot, frozen/mutable maps, proposed lines, degree,
pair conflict, delta/component, label/order/population, bool/float integers and
incorrect full-matrix scores at their designated checking stage. A wrong-stage
exception cannot satisfy a negative control.

The complete target audit must independently derive every224784 label, compare
every raw record and all category counts, revalidate all retained ties and
selected matrices, and disclose deduplicated graph counts separately if used.
No local census establishes target exclusion, a global or plateau minimum,
ergodicity, automorphism, complete trajectory, or unique support pairs.
