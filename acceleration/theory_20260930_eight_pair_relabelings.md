# Candidate eight-family signed-pair relabelings and clause transport

Preregistered before the production enumeration. No solver is part of this run.

The fixed99 root scaffold has a root, seven matched pairs of neighbors, and84
outer vertices indexed by one signed vertex from each of two different pairs.
A proposed relabeling fixes the root, maps old pair g to pi(g), flips its two
signs by epsilon[g], and maps each outer vertex by its two inner labels. This
is a change of names in the family; no automorphism of a target is assumed.

The raw eight-family scope has120 free outer pairs of the same sign sharing
each coordinate g in {0,1,2,3}, and none sharing each g in {4,5,6}. Its other1680
free pairs have disjoint coordinate supports. These counts are reconstructed
from the manifest. A signed-pair map preserving the free-pair set must preserve
the partition {0,1,2,3}/{4,5,6}. The proposed finite population is therefore all
4! times3! coordinate permutations preserving that partition and all128 sign
masks:18,432 maps. This is not a census claim about arbitrary permutations of
the99 vertices or automorphisms of any target graph. Independent review must
approve any stronger census interpretation.
