# Candidate simplified weighted-sum obstruction

All numbers are exact integers. Independent review is pending. This excludes only the fixed connected01 support if the matrix/scope and certificate are accepted.

For every one of the 4,067 retained color choices, the sum of its Gram-row weights plus the corresponding column one-hot weight is nonnegative. The weighted required right-hand side is -1. Thus a nonnegative selector solution cannot exist.

There are 382 nonzero Gram weights and 59 nonzero one-hot weights; the maximum absolute full weight is 50.

The complete coefficient table and all option checks are in weighted_Gram_rows.json and certificate.json. This is not a graph/core exclusion and is not yet a short unweighted combinatorial argument.
