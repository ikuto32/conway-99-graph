## Sound composition of the two necessary conditions

The prerequisite is `C-STAR-TRIANGLE-FILTER-18481` revision 1, bound to its
independently checked raw artifact hashes. Its 19,494 survivors are a sound
superset of the local stars that could occur in any target completion of the
fixed baseline18481 assignment. Reusing this claim does not require repeating
the complete matching enumeration. The original domains remain unchanged;
the filter records subsets of their original IDs and does not claim a new
complete enumeration of original stars.

For distinct outer vertices u,v, once one complete local neighborhood is
chosen for each, their edge decision must reciprocate. Furthermore the
intersection of those two 14-element full99 neighborhoods must have size 1
when adjacent, and size 2 otherwise. Both requirements are exact consequences
of the target parameters.

Suppose a choice at u has no compatible choice in the current domain at v.
By induction, every hypothetical target completion must still select a choice
from that current domain at v. The unsupported choice at u therefore cannot
occur and may be deleted. This proves soundness of a sequence of such
deletions when started from the independently sound matching survivors.
An empty domain would give a contradiction only for this fixed assignment.
Nonempty domains, even after all pair arcs are consistent, do not imply a
simultaneous compatible choice or a graph.

The simpler reciprocal-edge propagation uses the same argument with only
the two possible edge values. In this run it deletes no choices; the auditor
checks closure on every disjoint-support unknown pair instead of inferring it
from a zero-event producer log.

## Exact checking path

The auditor independently builds full neighborhoods as Python sets. It binds
the matching partition to the original candidate and domain hashes, validates
the partition of every original domain, parses the exact native input masks,
and verifies every packed-index-to-original-ID translation in both event and
survivor lists. Reduced-domain artifacts are required to retain
`original_complete_domains: false` and their explicit filtered scope.

Every translated pair-deletion event is replayed against the current domains.
For every current target choice, the auditor searches all support choices
until it finds one satisfying reciprocal adjacency and the exact set
intersection size. The independently computed unsupported list must equal
the entire recorded deletion list, with correct before/after counts.
After all events, every surviving choice is checked for support in every
other vertex's surviving domain: all 84×83 directed outer-vertex arcs.

Calibrated controls use noncontiguous original IDs. They include a sound
deletion, positive closed domains, an exhausted domain, and reciprocal-value
propagation. Corruptions exercise deletion of a supported choice, wrong
before/after counts, wrong final ID, an omitted necessary deletion, an
incorrect forced reciprocal value, and an altered original ID in the real
raw translation. Every corruption is rejected.

The independently checked outcome is 806 pair-deletion events, removing
4,159 choices from 19,494 matching survivors and leaving 15,335 choices across
84 nonempty domains. The checker made 15,060,016 exact set-based compatibility
tests including final closure. That count describes this checker only and is
not a performance comparison to the native producer.
