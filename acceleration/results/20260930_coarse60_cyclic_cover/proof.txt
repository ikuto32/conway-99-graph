For each saved orbit, its three coarse words use different fibres in every component.
A raw column selects row (fibre,component,bit), so rows from two orbit columns
can never coincide: rows from different components differ in component, and
rows from the same component differ in fibre, for either bit. Thus the three
columns are pairwise disjoint for every assignment of all 360 bits. The twenty
orbits cover all sixty columns exactly once. Every original factor therefore
has such a disjoint triangle partition, irrespective of its Gram equations.
The proposed triangle-selector extension would admit the twenty orbit selectors
and exact-one prefix values recomputed from them. All selected triples impose
zero opposite-bit conditions; unselected triples' implications are vacuous.
Therefore that extension alone is redundant for this particular coarse template.
This argument does not assert these are the triangles of a residual completion,
or provide residual D, a factor, or a target graph. No target automorphism is assumed.
