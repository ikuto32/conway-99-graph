Histogram generation enumerates bins0 through5 and solves the final6/7 counts
algebraically. For final values a and a+1, high=S-a*c, low=c-high. Both counts
must be nonnegative and their exact quadratic moment must agree. Necessary

the producer's balanced/concentrated exact envelopes. Every genuine residual
histogram satisfies these inequalities. Each prefix has at most one final pair,
so prefix order produces the exact same lexicographic complete population.

bitset transition. Every available copy is selected at most once. The focal
