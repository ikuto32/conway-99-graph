bounded 0..82. Set its upper bound u_i=1 when its mask cardinality is at least
three, justified by any contained triple packing row, or when the genuinely
checked equal-type pair is incompatible. Every p is bounded 0..1. Impose
p_i<=n_i and n_i<=u_i*p_i. For integer counts this gives p_i=1 iff n_i>0.

p_i+p_j<=1. Do not impose n_i+n_j<=1 on low types merely from incompatibility;
such a type can have multiplicity greater than one while its partner is absent.

two distinct exterior vertices. All other low-cardinality multiplicities remain
allowed. Types/cardinalities are never pruned from an earlier positive support.
