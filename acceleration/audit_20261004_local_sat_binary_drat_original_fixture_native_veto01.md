## Ctrl-Z positive does not test essential consumption

The original formula is the four clauses
`(13,14),(13,-14),(-13,14),(-13,-14)` with header `p cnf 14 4`.
Its eight-byte proof is `61 1d 00 61 1a 00 61 00`, namely additions
`(-14),(13),empty`.

All proposed positive steps are valid RUP. For `-14`, assume `14`; the two
clauses containing `-14` force opposite values of 13. After adding `-14`,
assumption `-13` contradicts `(13,14)`, so 13 is RUP. Empty is valid after
the two units.

However, the first completed unit `-14` already causes a propagation conflict:
`(13,14)` and `(-13,14)` become opposite unit clauses. A text-mode truncation
at the later byte `1a` leaves that valid first unit and an incomplete addition
prefix. The pinned parser stops on immediate proof EOF without inserting the
pending record (lines 1107-1111 and 1149), and default verification can reach
the refutation using only the first unit (lines 793-849). Consequently a
positive checker result on this original file does not establish that it
consumed the Ctrl-Z byte or the final records. Strict external record/EOF
decoding of the actual original bytes would not by itself expose a checker
that truncated its internal read.

on variables 1 and 2. The twelve-byte proof is
`61 0d 0a 00 61 05 00 61 02 00 61 00`, additions
`(-6,5),(-2),(1),empty`.

The first addition is an original clause. The valid contradiction uses only
the 1/2 component: assumption 2 validates -2, then -2 produces opposite
units on 1. The critical `0d 0a` sequence can therefore remain outside the
marked refutation dependency set.

Deleting `0d` converts the first addition into the unit 5. That addition is
not RUP: assumption -5 forces only -6 and leaves the 1/2 component nonunit.
It is not RAT on its sole pivot either: resolving with `(-5,8)` gives unit 8;
assumption -8 forces -5 then -6, without a propagation conflict. Nevertheless
the default backward checker is allowed to skip this unused unit. The
independent 1/2 contradiction marks its own clauses; the unmarked sensitive
addition is omitted at upstream lines 942-945. Its invalidity does not make
the original positive a sensitivity test. Forward checking was not requested
