518,160-clause arbitrary normalized triangle-core factor CNF. Both internal
matchings and the cross permutation remain free. Only M0/cross01/cross02
and canonical C0 are normalized. Exact Gram, margins, column caps and mixed
caps are included; no component restrictions, inherited incidence zeros,
fixed factor, nontrivial automorphism or residualD is assumed.

all-clause encoding gate and fresh arbitrary-core object-checker calibration.
