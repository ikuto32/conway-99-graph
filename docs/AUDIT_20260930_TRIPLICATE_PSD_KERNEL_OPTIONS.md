# Independent kernel-option redundancy argument

For a real factor F with Gram G and triple sums N, the already checked identity writes `M=3G-NN^T` as the sum of the within-triple column-difference outer products. If Mv=0 then `0=v^TMv` is the sum of the real squares of all difference projections. Each square is zero. Therefore every local triple in a realization must have equal projections of its three columns on every vector in ker(M). This is necessary, not sufficient.

The independent checker uses an explicit14-dimensional candidate space W. A vector in W has entries `v[f,a]=alpha[a]+beta[f]`; set beta0=0 to make the14 parameters unique. The basis consists of12 coordinate indicators across the three fibres and indicators of fibres1 and2. Restricting these vectors to the12 fibre0 positions and position0 in each of fibres1 and2 gives an invertible14-by14 minor. Thus dim(W)=14 independently of any producer elimination.

For the balanced baseline and each of the three exact profiles, the checker rebuilds M, verifies that every basis vector of W is annihilated, and establishes rank22 independently over the rationals. Since there are36 rows, the kernel has dimension14 and equals W. It also checks that each producer basis is complete and that every saved basis vector satisfies the explicit coordinate decomposition. Equality of the four kernels follows without trusting the producer's span comparison.

A local word on support S selects one fibre for each of the six support coordinates and exactly two of those coordinates in each fibre. Consequently its projection on v is

`sum_(a in S) alpha[a] + 2beta1 + 2beta2`.

This expression is independent of the chosen word. Hence every within-support column difference is orthogonal to W, even before choosing a local triple. The complete20×90 baseline projection table and every saved local-option projection/difference are checked directly as well. It follows that these three exact kernels cannot remove any of their original local options. The computed6444 count is the sum of the three independently reconstructed class populations, not a count of globally compatible factors.

The argument about W applies to its stated vector space and this word convention. The audit does not assert that every other count profile has kernel exactly W; another profile could have additional kernel directions. Generic SRG243 and synthetic factor controls use their own Grams. No global Gram realization, cross-group column-cap compatibility, residual D, target existence/exclusion or novelty follows from this local redundancy result.
