# Independent transport of two UNKNOWN traces

This audit will authenticate exactly the two retained host partial traces from
the separately reviewed standalone and count-coupled direct-cell runs. The
original ext4 paths were absent at the later outcome audit. That observation
remains part of the record; transport does not claim to restore their historical
availability or convert either partial trace into a contradiction certificate.

The manifest and completed packaging summary must be frozen and supplied by hash
before execution. An independently authored standard-library reader will check
the exact two variant identities against their outcome gates, all ordered part
indices/offsets, compressed and decompressed lengths/hashes, single gzip-member
boundaries, deterministic gzip headers, and every recovered byte against the
retained authenticated host original. Whole-stream hashes and totals must agree
with the manifest and prior outcome identities. No producer or recovery code is
imported and no solver or DRAT checker is called.

Research chunks have at most 8 MiB uncompressed bytes, and every compressed public
part must be below 10 MiB. Controls use a separate synthetic binary stream with
at least two chunks. The complete positive reconstruction must pass, while
missing, duplicated or reordered parts, incorrect offsets, sizes and hashes,
unsafe paths, truncation, bad CRC, extra gzip members and self-consistent changed
payloads must fail. Controls are not research proofs.

The result is byte transport only, with no feasibility, exclusion, coverage,
performance, external-review or public-publication assertion. Existing originals,
packages, failed audit versions and outcome gates remain unchanged. A failed new
audit is preserved separately rather than overwriting its records.
