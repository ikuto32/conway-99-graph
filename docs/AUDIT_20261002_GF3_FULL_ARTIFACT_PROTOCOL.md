# Original literal GF(3) endpoint protocol, 2026-10-02

This new checker is frozen and calibrated before any full scientific GF(3)
output is inspected. It shares the separately authored scalar helper and
deadline code, and imports no producer/packed solver code. Calibration covers
five fixture operators, four complete primal sets, one original weighted row
relation and deliberate vector, coefficient/sign, RHS, relation, literal record,
stream digest and content-three scope corruptions. Missing or malformed records
must fail at a stated stage rather than downstream incidental failure.

For a full output, authenticate the frozen 23,019-column 85,874-row raw model,
input-case and summary, all declared inputs/outputs, checker source and prior
calibration. Reconstruct every positive gcd over literal integer coefficients
and all three RHS components; check exact division and roundtrip, every raw and
divided row hash, complete normalized JSONL bytes, original modular sparse bytes
and content census. Normalization is identity evidence only: every certificate
sum uses the original signed coefficients, including duplicated terms.

Accept three primals only after all 257,622 original row-component sums vanish
modulo three. Accept a relation only after a complete original-row coefficient
list gives zero in all 23,019 columns and the reported nonzero three-component
affine RHS. A changed full vector coordinate must be rejected. Checkpoint hashes
identify resume state; full checkpoint basis semantics are not part of a primal
or relation verdict. Availability is tracked separately from hash identities.

Calibration allocation is 60 seconds outer / 40 worker / 20 reserve for five
tiny operators and finite negative controls. A subsequent complete replay uses
300 outer / 260 worker / 40 reserve, based on the previous GF(2) full scalar check
and fewer than one million raw terms. No numerical or native solver is invoked,
and no automatic retry or budget extension is permitted. Preserve every failure.
An unfinished native output receives no mathematical certificate status.

The endpoint verdict concerns the literal finite-field system. A model-level
integer exclusion additionally needs independently checked necessary-model
coverage and explicit UNKNOWN prism-free premise. Primal consistency implies
neither exact rational/integer nonnegative feasibility nor graph realization.
No full-operator rank is certified and no unrestricted Conway-99 conclusion is
drawn by this checker. Root-led ledger binding and public availability audit are
separate from this artifact check.
