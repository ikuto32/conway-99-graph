# Independent finite twelve-order premise-collection audit

This audit leaves the original fourteen-pattern gate unchanged. It checks a
new finite collection produced by `/root`, using previously frozen independent
phase and certificate checkers authored by `/root/structural_attack`. No
discovery routine is imported. General necessity and full five-group pair-row
dependency rules remain the mathematical premises; no target automorphism or
unrestricted balanced-support assumption is introduced.

The declared orders are descending, ascending, then ten permutations generated
by one Python Random(0) shuffle stream. The verifier regenerates that ordering
metadata and checks every saved order and checkpoint. That RNG check is a
repeat of the declared engineering protocol, not the independent mathematical
checking path. Every one of the240recorded row selections and span decisions
is checked through the separate rank/augmented-rank method. Identical retained
row sets may share a cached exact calculation; both record count and distinct
mathematical-input count are reported.

All twelve final certificates are checked by literal row-combination arithmetic
against independently reconstructed120-variable equations. Every used row has
its complete parity premises retained; the violated mixed-group condition is
retained too. Thus each resulting partial pattern assignment is impossible for
every balanced full-Gram factor matching it, regardless of the other groups.
The exact selector clauses are reconstructed independently, deduplicated by
literal identity, and compared pairwise for proper subsumption. No clause is
added to a CNF and no solver is called.

The original full twenty-pattern projection belongs to every excluded partial
assignment. Therefore the excluded families overlap. Their counts must not be
added as disjoint search coverage. The smallest group count in these twelve
trials is only the best of this declared batch; a global minimum or lower bound
does not follow. Producer timing is not independently replayed as a performance
claim. The audit saves its own execution receipt and actual elapsed time.

Known positive certificate and rank controls are reused from the hash-bound
independent helper gate. Fresh corruptions alter a final row combination, omit
a required pair dependency, change reported stage/best counts, and break an
order permutation. Every source, result, checkpoint and prior failure remains
unchanged. The finite collection can receive a scoped ledger claim after the
root's integration; this checker does not edit the ledger.
