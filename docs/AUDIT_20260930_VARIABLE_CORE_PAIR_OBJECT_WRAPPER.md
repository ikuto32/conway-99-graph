# Independent review of the ordered-pair SAT-object wrapper

Reviewer: `/root/eight_domain_audit`; wrapper author and orbit-encoding producer:
`/root/structural_attack`. The wrapper does not independently approve its own
encoding or scientific output. Its frozen pending-review status is retained.

I reviewed the complete `bind`, `sat`, and `selected_pair` paths. Admission
requires the independently checked normalization report's exact hash and status,
the unchanged base-object checker calibration, all transitive input hashes,
and exact extended CNF/extension/coverage and base-model/scope identities.
The wrapper reconstructs the suffix and compares the complete original clause
body and extension bytes. These checks supplement the independent normalization
gate; they do not substitute for its coverage proof.

For a research SAT result, it parses every114484 signed assignment value and
all native model lines, requiring exact agreement. It evaluates all561121
actual enlarged clauses, projects precisely IDs1..110904, rechecks all518160
base clauses, then invokes the frozen independent base decoder. That decoder
constructs and validates the raw M1/M2/P matrices, integer factor Gram/margins,
mixed and column caps, and the partial99 graph. Thus the wrapper does not accept
an assignment solely because its selector or a producer-decoded matrix passes.
It requires exactly one selector and checks all132 matching-edge variables,
both decoded matching matrices, and the stable representative index against
the independently authenticated3580-pair census. Optional producer decoding
is compared to the independently constructed object. Saved raw factors remain
partial graph objects; residual D is not supplied or inferred.

The separate review script authenticates the wrapper calibration and all raw
saved controls. It independently parses the complete synthetic native model
and evaluates every synthetic CNF clause, including the actual42961-clause
selector suffix. Synthetic prefix clauses are codec controls, not the research
formula and not a research SAT witness. It independently checks the raw rook9
fixture over the integers and separately exercises all3580 selected-pair cases,
with both raw matrices supplied, plus fresh selector, matrix, native, clause,
and gate corruptions. Direct calls into the wrapper during these adversarial
controls are explicitly black-box tests of the reviewed implementation; they
are not an independent mathematical derivation. Raw synthetic checking and
the written path review are separate from those calls.

Trust is shared with the previously independently calibrated base-object
checker and its native/JSON/CNF parsers. This review does not create a second
independent implementation of those parsers or claim universal software
correctness. No complete36-row research factor exists in this calibration.
Any future actual candidate still requires its bound raw artifacts to be
checked, and the wrapper retains `CHECK_PASS_PENDING_REVIEW` until that occurs.
No solver is launched by this review. No target-resolution claim is made.
