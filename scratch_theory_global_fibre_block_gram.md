## 1. The incidence matrix

For every root `r`, its 84 distance-two vertices split into the 21 support
fibres of size four.  Let `M` have one row for each `(r,fibre)` and one
column for each graph vertex.  Thus `M` is `2079 x 99`, every row has four
ones, and every column has 84 ones: a vertex occurs once for every
nonneighbour chosen as root.

Put `H=M^T M`.  Direct double counting gives

```text
H_xx                         = 84,
H 1                          = 336 1,
tr H                         = 8316,
sum_(x<y) H_xy               = 2079 binom(4,2) = 12474.
```

If

```text
T = sum_r E0(r),
```

then every edge inside a root fibre contributes once to both sides, so

```text
sum_(x~y) H_xy               = T,
sum_(x nonadjacent y) H_xy   = 12474-T.                 (1)
```

The rooted fibre equations give `0<=e_F<=4`, hence `0<=T<=8316`.

## 2. Primitive projector traces


Using `tr(AH)=2T` and `tr(JH)=1^T H 1=33264` gives exactly

```text

tr(P-4 H)  = 4092-2T/7,
tr(P3 H)   = 3888+2T/7.                                 (2)
```

All three are strictly positive throughout `0<=T<=8316`, so trace

Since `H` is positive semidefinite and is a nonnegative matrix of constant
row sum 336,

```text
0 <= H <= 336 I,
25 <= rank(H) <= 99.                                    (3)
```

The lower rank bound is only `ceil(tr(H)/336)=25`.

## 3. Pinching is exactly relation-wise Jensen

The Frobenius projection of `H` onto the three-dimensional Bose--Mesner
algebra is

```text
Hbar = 84I + (T/693)A
           + ((12474-T)/4158)(J-I-A).                   (4)
```

Its eigenvalues on the three primitive spaces are

```text
336,  72+T/189,  93-T/154.                              (5)
```

Therefore

```text
tr(H^2)
 >= 336^2 + 54(72+T/189)^2 + 44(93-T/154)^2.            (6)
```

This is not an independent spectral improvement.  It is identically the
continuous entry-wise Jensen bound

```text
99*84^2 + 2[T^2/693 + (12474-T)^2/4158],                (7)
```

and the exact Pythagorean identity is

```text
||H||_F^2 = ||Hbar||_F^2 + ||H-Hbar||_F^2.              (8)
```

Thus primitive pinching only measures deviation from constant entries on
the diagonal, edge, and nonedge relations.

## 4. Summing the rooted support projectors

There is a second exact incidence calculation which retains the seven
root-neighbour groups.  Let `U` have rows `(r,g)`, with `g` one of the seven
triangles in `N(r)`, and put `U[(r,g),x]=1` when `x` is an outer vertex at
`r` whose two-group support contains `g`.  Thus `U` is `693 x 99`, has row
weight 24 and column weight 168.  Counting the common supported groups of
two vertices gives

```text
U^T U = 126I-18A+42J,                                  (A)
```

whose diagonal, edge, and nonedge entries are respectively `168,24,42`.
If `h=H_xy`, then the roots at which the two supports are equal, overlap in
one group, or are disjoint occur in the following exact numbers:

| pair type | equal | one-group overlap | disjoint |
|---|---:|---:|---:|
| `x~y` | `h` | `24-2h` | `48+h` |
| `x nonadjacent y` | `h` | `42-2h` | `29+h` |

In particular, the fibre codegrees satisfy the sharper pointwise caps

```text
H_xy <= 12 on edges,       H_xy <= 21 on nonedges.      (B)
```

For a fixed root, project the 21-dimensional fibre-constant space away
from the unsigned `K7` vertex-incidence space.  The support projector has
entries `2/3,-2/15,1/15` according as two `K7` edges are equal, overlap, or
are disjoint, and has rank 14.  After lifting each support coordinate to
its four vertices and summing the resulting positive semidefinite
projectors `P_r`, one obtains the exact matrix identity

```text
G := sum_r P_r
   = H/4-7I-(11/12)(J-I-A) >= 0,                        (C)
G1=0,  tr G=1386,  0<=G<=84I.
```

The operator bound gives only `rank(G)>=17`.

Its primitive traces and square are

```text
tr(P3 G)    = 792+T/14,
tr(P-4 G)   = 594-T/14,
tr(G^2)     = K_fb/4+33033/2+11T/12.                   (D)
```

Primitive-space pinching of `G` yields

```text
K_fb >= 12474-3T+T^2/1188.                             (E)
```

This appealing PSD inequality is exactly the continuous collision Jensen
bound already implicit in (7).  The integer bound below is never weaker
and can exceed (E) by as much as 594.  At the critical boundary `T=0`,

```text
G0=14I-(J-I-A)/6
```

has eigenvalues `0,44/3,27/2` on the constant, `3`, and `-4` spaces.  Thus
the additional root-group projector also remains positive semidefinite at
zero `E0`.  Like `H`, `G` has already summed over the 99 root classes and
therefore has no access to the row collision `sum_r E0(r)^2`.

## 5. Integer entry energy

For nonnegative integers in `m` cells with sum `S=mq+r`, `0<=r<m`, define

```text
sqmin(S,m)=m q^2+r(2q+1).
```

Integer codegrees sharpen (7) to

```text
tr(H^2) >= 99*84^2
             +2[sqmin(T,693)+sqmin(12474-T,4158)].       (9)
```

The exact extra rounding gap above (6) is

```text
2[r_E(693-r_E)/693+r_N(4158-r_N)/4158],                 (10)
```

where `r_E` and `r_N` are the two division remainders.  It vanishes exactly
at

```text
T=0, 4158, 8316,
```

and its maximum is 2376, at `T=1782,2376,5940,6534`.
This is exact extra information, but it does not exclude `T=0`.

There is also a finite-field necessary condition.  Because `H=M^T M` and
every diagonal entry is the even number 84, `H mod 2` is alternating;
therefore its binary rank is even and at most 98, and `H1=0 mod 2`.
The boundary matrix below satisfies this condition.

The local row split does not improve (9).  Set

```text
t_x=sum_(y~x) H_xy,     sum_x t_x=2T,     0<=t_x<=252.
```

For fixed integer `t_x`, distributing a row over its 14 edge and 84
nonedge positions gives

```text
84^2+sqmin(t_x,14)+sqmin(252-t_x,84).
```

Exact minimization over all 99 integers `t_x` with sum `2T` was performed
for every `T=0,...,8316`; it equals (9) identically.  Thus localizing the
integer energy by vertices adds no hidden second-moment constraint.

Two Schur-product tests also close without improvement.  Since every
fibre row is binary and each vertex occurs in 84 rows,

```text
B3=H o P3, B-4=H o P-4 satisfy 0<=B3,B-4<=84I.
```

Their row sums are `432/11+t_x/7` and `124/3-t_x/7`.  Applying
`B^2<=84B` gives

```text
sum_x t_x^2 <= (93731904+840T)/11,
sum_x t_x^2 <= 8555008-56T/3.                           (F)
```

For every integral `T`, both are weaker than the elementary box maximum
for `99` integers in `[0,252]` with sum `2T`.  Likewise `H o H>=0` reduces,
after rowwise Jensen, exactly to (9).

## 6. A universal fibre-collision inequality

Define a fibre-block collision count (written `K_fb` to distinguish it from
the unrelated support-union `K` in the triangle-flag note)

```text
K_fb = sum_(x<y) binom(H_xy,2).
```

Equivalently, if `B,C` range over distinct fibre rows,

```text
K_fb = sum_(B<C) binom(|B intersect C|,2).               (11)
```

Indeed, both sides choose a vertex pair and two fibre blocks containing it.
Since distinct fibres at one root are disjoint, every nonzero term in (11)
uses two different roots.

The entry identity and integer lower bound are

```text
tr(H^2) = 723492+4K_fb,                                 (12)

K_fb >= cmin(T,693)+cmin(12474-T,4158),                 (13)
```

where

```text
cmin(S,m)=m binom(q,2)+rq,  S=mq+r.
```

Exhausting all integral `T` gives the new parameter-free consequence

```text
K_fb >= 10395.                                          (14)
```

The numerical lower bound is equal to 10395 for every
`1386<=T<=2079`.  At `T=0`, the stronger value is `K_fb>=12474`.

This is a genuine universal integer fibre-flag collision inequality.  It is
not a positive lower bound on `T`; its equality interval explicitly crosses
many possible positive values and the `T=0` specialization remains feasible
at the Gram level.

## 7. Exact four-root location of the collision count

A term of (11) chooses distinct rooted fibres `(r,F),(s,G)` and a shared
pair `{x,y}`.  Fibre membership forces

```text
rx, ry, sx, sy all to be nonedges.                       (15)
```

On the four vertices `r,s,x,y`, only `rs` and `xy` may be edges.  Under the
existing unlabelled four-root mask convention, the possibilities are

| `rs` | `xy` | root mask |
|---:|---:|---:|
| 0 | 0 | 0 |
| 1 | 0 | 1 |
| 0 | 1 | 1 |
| 1 | 1 | 12 (`2K2`) |

Root mask 3 (`P3` plus an isolated vertex) cannot occur in this collision
family.  Thus (14) lives in fibre-membership-enriched flags over masks
0, 1, and 12.  The known negative full covariance block at mask 12 is a
plausible place to seek more strength, but its raw root-mask count does not
evaluate `K_fb`: a new lift retaining both fibre memberships is required.
There is no direct coupling to the mask-3 negative direction.

## 8. What `H` says about the second moment

Let `X` be the binary `99 x 693` matrix

```text
X[r,xy]=1 iff edge xy lies inside one fibre at root r.
```

Its row sums are `E0(r)`, while its column sums are the edge entries `H_xy`.
Consequently

```text
sum_r E0(r)^2
  = T+2 sum_r binom(E0(r),2),                            (16)

sum_(xy edge) H_xy^2
  = T+2 sum_(xy edge) binom(H_xy,2).                     (17)
```

These are row collisions and column collisions of the same binary matrix,
not equal quantities.  The Gram `H` retains the column sums but forgets the
partition of its 2079 defining rows into 99 roots.  Hence it does not
determine `sum E0(r)^2`.

The universal integer bounds visible from `T` alone remain

```text
sqmin(T,99) <= sum_r E0(r)^2
             <= 84^2 floor(T/84)+(T mod 84)^2.           (18)
```

The upper bound uses `0<=E0(r)<=84`.  At `T=0`, both sides are zero, so (18)
cannot provide a positive pointwise bound.

There is, however, a useful pointwise motif bridge.  Put

```text
t_x = sum_(y~x) H_xy = (AH)_xx.
```

Let `P_x` count induced triangular prisms containing `x`, let `R_x=D(x)`
count `H_delta` copies rooted at `x`, and let `L_x` count `H_delta` copies in
which `x` is an endpoint of the distinguished diagonal chord.  A prism has
one rooted side event at each of its six vertices and each vertex is an
endpoint of two of the six internal edges.  An `H_delta` has one intrinsic
root and two chord endpoints.  Therefore

```text
t_x   = 2P_x+L_x,
E0(x) = P_x+R_x,
t_x   = 2E0(x)+L_x-2R_x.                                (19)
```

Also `sum L_x=2 sum R_x`, recovering `sum t_x=2T`.  Equation (19) is a new
pointwise bridge, but no pointwise comparison between the chord-endpoint and
root roles is currently known.  Without that extra cross-root balance it
does not yield an `E0(x)` lower bound.

Squaring (19) makes the obstruction explicit:

```text
sum_x t_x^2 = 4 sum_x E0(x)^2
              +sum_x (L_x-2R_x)^2
              +4 sum_x E0(x)(L_x-2R_x).                (19a)
```

The last term has no fixed sign.  The global identity `sum L=2 sum R`
cancels only the corresponding first moment, so neither (19a) nor the
Schur upper bounds (F) transfer to a lower bound on `sum E0^2`.

There is also an exact edge-role decomposition of the collision energy.
For an edge `e`, write

```text
H_e=p_e+d_e,
```

where `p_e` counts its prism triangle-edge roles and `d_e` its `H_delta`
chord roles, and put

```text
K_edge=sum_(e edge) binom(H_e,2),
K_non =sum_(f nonedge) binom(H_f,2).
```

Then `K_fb=K_edge+K_non`.  If

```text
W_inc=sum_x sum_(e<f incident with x) H_e H_f,
```

direct expansion gives

```text
sum_x t_x^2=2T+4K_edge+2W_inc.                          (G)
```

Thus `sum t_x^2>=2T+4cmin(T,693)`, as well as the direct integer Cauchy
bound `sqmin(2T,99)`.  Neither is positive at `T=0`: all of the compulsory
collision mass `K_fb>=12474` can lie in `K_non`, which is invisible to the
rooted prism/chord events.

## 10. Exact Gram-level boundary

Consider

```text
H0 = 84I+3(J-I-A).                                      (23)
```

It has diagonal 84, edge entries zero, nonedge entries three, row sum 336,
and the required relation totals for `T=0`.  Its eigenvalues are

```text
336^1, 72^54, 93^44.
```

Thus `H0` is integer, entrywise nonnegative, positive definite of full rank
99, satisfies every projector trace, the operator bound, the mod-2
condition, and attains equality in both pinching and integer energy.  Two
more integral positive-definite relation-algebra controls occur at

```text
T=4158: H=84I+6A+2(J-I-A),
T=8316: H=84I+12A+(J-I-A).
```

At the still weaker event-incidence margin, there is also a completely
explicit integral `T=0` model.  Take 4158 abstract nonedge-event columns
labelled `(i,j)` with `i in Z_99`, `0<=j<42`, and make column `(i,j)`
incident with roots

```text
{i,i+1,i+j+2} mod 99.
```

This binary matrix has constant row degree 126 and column degree 3.  It
therefore realizes `K_non=4158*binom(3,2)=12474`, while its edge-event rows
have `E0=0` and `sum E0^2=0`.  The columns are deliberately abstract: this
is a sharp marginal countermodel, not an assignment to actual graph
nonedges or a decomposition into fibre four-sets.

This proves a precise limitation: no positive global or pointwise `E0`
lower bound follows from PSD, rank, the stated projector traces, nonnegative
integer entries, relation sums, or their energy/pinching consequences alone.

One caveat is essential.  We do **not** claim a binary row-weight-four
factorization of `H0`.  Such a factorization would be a 3-fold `K4`
decomposition of the complement graph: every row would be an independent
4-set of `G`, and every nonedge pair would occur in exactly three rows.  Its
basic divisibility conditions do pass:

```text
3*4158/6 = 2079 blocks,
3*84/3   = 84 blocks through each vertex.
```

The actual fibre construction requires much more: these rows must split
into 99 labelled classes of 21 disjoint blocks, each covering the complement
of one closed root neighbourhood, and each block must have the correct two-
triangle support.  `H` forgets precisely this information.

## Conclusion

The global incidence Gram yields one clean universal inequality,
`K_fb>=10395`, the sharp fibre-codegree caps (B), and the pointwise role
identity (19).  The summed root-group projector (C) is exact, but its
trace-square inequality is precisely the continuous relaxation of the
integer collision cut.  The collision flags lie in fibre-enriched
four-root masks 0, 1, and 12.  None of these consequences produces a
positive `E0` lower bound.  The full-rank integer PSD control (23) and the
explicit zero-event marginal model show that the remaining work must use
binary four-set factorization, its 99 parallel root partitions, or an
explicit lifted mask-12 covariance constraint—not another unlifted
trace/rank/pinching argument.
